About

Hi! I'm Kehinde

I'm the creator behind FLORAL VOID, a Northeast Ohio furry creator with a hearing impairment and a passion for character-centric art. Welcome to my corner of the world!

I've always been an artsy person, and it brings me great joy to share my talents with others and bring happiness and excitement into their world. Using my knowledge of animation, illustration and even tailoring, I bring other people's visions of characters to life with a uniquely animated spark.

After earning my Animation degree in 2021, I turned this business into a full-time venture. I keep striving for the most unique, most resilient and most adorable results possible in every form of art I pursue, especially fursuits. It has been quite the journey since our humble beginnings in 2019, and I look forward to continuing to give back to the amazing furry fandom.

Want to be part of the magic? Come join the FLORAL VOID Family!
