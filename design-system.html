06 · Form fields

Commission form style

Please enter a complete email address.
Fursona Design, VRChat Avatar and VTuber Avatar are all open for commissions. They are priced by quote, so choose one and tell us what you need.

Rules: every field has a visible label; inputs are 16px (prevents zoom on iPhone) and 48px tall; errors use red plus an icon and text; focus shows a gold ring; the button shows a loading state and only reports success after the server confirms the email was sent.

The Service list includes every service, grouped: the four fursuit tiers (confirmed prices; Full Suit stays a placeholder), then Fursona Design, VRChat Avatar and VTuber Avatar, then Other / Custom Request. Only fursuits show prices; every other option reads "Request a Quote" and a helper line states these services are available.

07 · Motion

Subtle, predictable, optional.

InteractionMotion
Section revealfade + rise 20px, 700ms
Buttonscolour swap + 2px lift, 200ms
Cardslift 6px, 400ms
Photoszoom to 103%, 700ms
Gallery filterfade out/in, 300ms
Mobile menuslide + fade, 400ms
Lightboxfade + scale 0.98→1, 300ms

Easing is cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1) (smooth ease-out). Only transform and opacity are animated, so nothing shifts the layout.

No bouncing, spinning, particles or parallax. With “reduce motion” enabled in the device, all of it switches off. Content is visible without JavaScript; reveals only enhance it.

Scroll reveal demo: this box rises into place as it enters the screen.
